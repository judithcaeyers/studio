FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I automate my administration?

List recurring tasks, their inputs, steps and outputs. Start with a frequent, predictable, time-consuming task.

How can I spend less time on repetitive tasks?

Remove unnecessary steps, standardise the remaining workflow and automate where appropriate. That avoids automating needless complexity.

Can I automate Excel files?

Yes. Examples include validating data, merging tables, updating charts and generating files. Formulas, Power Query or scripts may be appropriate.

Can reports be generated automatically?

Yes, if source data is accessible and sufficiently structured. We can generate standard reports and flag exceptions for review.

Can quotes be prepared automatically?

Yes. With structured pricing rules, enquiry data and a template, a tool can prepare a quote for you to review before sending.

Can I combine data from several applications automatically?

Often, through exports or APIs. We check fields, permissions and data quality and build in validation.

Can I commission a custom application for my business?

Yes. An internal web tool or small software product can solve a specific workflow. We start with a clearly scoped prototype.

How much does custom software cost for a small business?

Cost depends on features, integrations, security and maintenance. A simple tool and a business-critical system have very different scopes; we quote in advance.

Do I always need AI to automate processes?

No. When rules are fixed, conventional automation is often more predictable and economical. AI is particularly useful for less structured information.

Can I automate without switching software?

Sometimes existing integrations, exports or a small supplementary tool are enough. We first assess whether you can keep your current software.
