FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which AI training is right for my company?

It depends on your team and goals. Our hands-on workshops focus on real workflows and tangible applications, not just prompts.

Can I arrange an AI workshop for my staff?

Yes. We tailor the content to your sector, experience and recurring tasks so participants can work with their own examples.

How much does a tailored AI workshop cost?

Pricing depends on participant numbers, duration, preparation and support. Request a proposal with your audience and location.

Are there AI workshops for people without coding experience?

Yes. We start by understanding a task and defining clear instructions. Coding experience is not required for the introductory workshop.

How can my staff learn to work more efficiently with AI?

Have them select a task, run a small test and review the output. This helps them recognise both capabilities and limitations.

Can I build a tool during an AI workshop?

That is the aim of the hands-on workshop: a first prototype for a scoped task. Production readiness and complex integrations may require follow-up.

What is the difference between ChatGPT training and an AI automation workshop?

ChatGPT training often focuses on using a conversational assistant. We start from a workflow and explore how to build a reusable tool for it.

Do you offer on-site AI workshops?

Yes, by arrangement. We agree on location, group size, internet access and equipment in advance.
