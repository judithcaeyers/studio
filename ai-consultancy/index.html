FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I integrate AI into my business?

Map your processes first. Choose a repetitive task with a clear output, test a small solution using representative data and measure quality and time savings before scaling.

Where should a small business start with AI?

Start with a frequent task, such as summarising enquiries or extracting information from documents. We can assess what is feasible with your existing resources.

What can AI actually do for my business?

AI can classify text, extract information from documents or draft responses. The right use case depends on your processes, data and accuracy requirements.

Which business processes can I automate with AI?

Enquiry handling, quote preparation, document extraction and report summaries are possible uses. Predictable steps can often use conventional automation.

How much does AI implementation cost for a small business?

Cost depends on scope, data, integrations, security and maintenance. We define the pilot and estimate both one-off and recurring costs before starting.

Can I use AI without technical expertise?

Yes. A well-designed tool can fit your existing workflow. We provide guidance on how to use it and when human review remains necessary.

How long does AI implementation take?

A small proof of concept can be developed quickly; a dependable production integration requires more time for testing, access control and rollout. Timing depends on scope.

Can AI integrate with my existing software?

Often, through APIs, exports or existing integrations. We first check available access, data and constraints.

How do I know whether an AI investment will pay off?

Measure current time costs and errors, estimate feasible improvements and compare them with build, licence and maintenance costs. Freed-up time is not automatically cash savings.

What does an AI consultant do?

An AI consultant assesses opportunities and risks, helps choose use cases, guides tests and can support implementation. At Rho & Sigma we combine that with design and development.

How can I use AI safely with business data?

We consider data minimisation, access rights, processor agreements, retention and human oversight. Sensitive data should not simply be entered into public AI tools.

Is AI useful for a sole trader?

Yes, particularly if a recurring task takes substantial time. A small, simple solution may be more valuable than a large AI programme.
