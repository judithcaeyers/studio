Frequently asked questions

What is service design?

Service design examines how customers and staff experience a service, from first contact to follow-up, and redesigns the steps and tools that support it.

Can you design a space or retail concept?

We can research and design spatial concepts and customer experience. Technical design, permits and execution are agreed with qualified partners for each project.

Can you build a website for my small business?

Yes. We design around your offer, identity and goals. A focused one-page site starts at €995 excluding VAT; extra features are quoted separately.
