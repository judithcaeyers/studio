FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I use AI in my hospitality business?

Consider summarising enquiries, drafting replies or structuring order information. For calculations such as quantities, fixed rules are often more dependable.

How can I automate catering enquiries?

A tool like Bob can prepare incoming enquiries, highlight missing information and draft follow-ups. You decide what gets sent.

Can AI prepare my emails and quotes?

Yes, with clear business rules, pricing data and review before sending. Bob is designed around that workflow.

Can recipes be scaled automatically?

Yes. A tool can scale ingredients by portions and units. Cooking losses, rounding and recipe technique still need consideration.

Can orders automatically become shopping lists?

Yes, when orders and recipes are sufficiently structured. A tool can combine ingredients, convert units and highlight exceptions.

How can I simplify my catering administration?

Map the steps from enquiry to delivery. Choose one bottleneck, such as quote preparation, production planning or purchasing lists.

Which digital tools are useful for caterers?

It depends on your workflow. Options include inbox support, ordering platforms, recipe calculations, stock management and internal planning tools. We help identify what is useful.
