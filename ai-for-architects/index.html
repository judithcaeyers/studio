FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I use AI in my architecture practice?

Start with recurring administrative tasks, such as extracting document information or drafting reports. Keep design decisions and technical checks with responsible professionals.

Which AI tools are useful for architects?

It depends on the task: language models for text, scripts for data processing and design software for visualisation. Choose based on reliability, privacy and workflow fit.

Can site reports be automated?

A tool can structure notes, fill standard sections and draft reports. The responsible professional verifies content and technical observations.

Can bills of quantities be processed automatically?

Structured quantity data can be merged, validated or converted to a template. Quantities and contractual interpretation require review.

How can I automate drawing management?

Drawing registers and revision data can be processed through exports or integrations into status reports and dashboards, with checks for missing or unexpected data.

How can architects spend less time on administration?

Inventory recurring documentation, reporting and tracking tasks. Start with a task that has clear rules and frequent repetition.

Are there AI workshops specifically for architects?

Yes. Rho & Sigma offers hands-on workshops where design teams analyse their own tasks and build a first solution.

How can I use AI without giving up my creative process?

Automate the steps you do not want to repeat. Use AI for preparation and alternatives, while retaining authorship, design decisions and final review.
