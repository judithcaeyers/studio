06 / Good to know

A few things you might wonder.

Is €995 the total price?

It's the starting price, excluding VAT, for a focused one-page website. Before work starts you'll get a clear quote with the full scope. Domain, hosting and optional extras are separate.

Do I need to write all the text?

You know your business best, so we'll need input about your services and story. I can help organise and sharpen the content. Extensive copywriting can be quoted separately.

What about photos?

Great photography makes a big difference. I can use suitable images you already have or discuss alternatives. For architecture and other visual projects, I can also help arrange a photographer at an additional cost.

Can my website do more than one page?

Absolutely. Extra pages, booking, small shops, multilingual content or smarter enquiry flows are possible. I quote them separately so you only pay for what your business actually needs.

How long does it take?

That depends on the scope and how quickly we have the necessary content. I'll agree on a realistic timeline with you before starting, rather than promising a launch date we can't guarantee.
